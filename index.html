What We Found

Here is what your answers and our checks indicate. We do not determine whether the rental is legitimate or fraudulent. Use the findings below to investigate further before making any payment.

    Next Steps

    1. Check the registered property owner

      Search the address on tinglysning.dk and check who is registered as the owner. If the owner's name does not match the landlord, ask for written authorization from the owner confirming that the property may be sublet.

    2. Perform a reverse image search

      Search the listing's images using Google Lens or TinEye . If the same images appear in other listings, such as Airbnb or listings in another city, investigate whether they have been reused.

    3. Compare the rent with similar properties

      Find comparable properties with a similar location, size, and number of rooms, for example on BoligPortal . A price substantially below comparable listings can be a warning sign.

    4. Do not pay before reviewing a rental contract

      Do not pay a deposit or rent before receiving and reviewing a signed rental agreement, and preferably after viewing the property. Use a traceable bank transfer where possible. Avoid cash payments or services such as Western Union.

    5. Report the listing if something does not match

      Use the platform's "Report listing" function. If you have already transferred money, contact your bank immediately and report the incident to the police at politi.dk .

    RentalTrace provides information only and cannot guarantee that a rental property or landlord is legitimate.
